What you're looking at

This is a faithful HTML mockup of the DockWidgetPluginGui that mounts inside Roblox Studio after you install RoTree.rbxm. Colors, spacing, fonts, and component layout come directly from plugin/src/UI/Theme.luau and plugin/src/UI/MainWindow.luau — what you see is what you'll get, modulo Roblox's slightly different font rendering.

What each card does

  • Bridge — green dot when rotree serve (or VS Code) is reachable on localhost:34872. "Test connection" pings it.
  • Exports — manual exports + the two toggles. Watch mode = re-export on every DataModel change (event-driven + debounced).
  • Last export — stats from the most recent payload.
  • AI Context — regenerate AI_CONTEXT.md or reveal .rotree/.
  • Rojo — shows the diff status against your default.project.json.
  • Patch Safety — review pending patches; toggle to let the AI auto-apply non-critical patches.
  • Appearance — light / dark.

Try the toggles

The toggles on this page are live — tap "Dark mode" on the preview itself, or the "Light / Dark" pill at the top of the page. Both work and persist via localStorage.
